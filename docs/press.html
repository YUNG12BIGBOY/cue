cue for Windows

Everything you need to write about cue: boilerplate, facts, screenshots and brand guidelines.

Boilerplate

Short (50 words)

cue for Windows is a free, open-source AI copilot that floats above every app. It can read your screen, hear your microphone and meeting audio, and answer in real time using Dahl, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or a local model. No account and no telemetry.

Long (100 words)

cue for Windows is a free, open-source AI copilot overlay built for Windows 11 and Windows 10 2004+. It floats above other applications and combines three inputs — the screen, the microphone, and system audio from the other side of a meeting — to answer questions in real time. This Windows fork adds first-class Dahl and OpenRouter providers, chat text size and boldness controls, a Clear chat button, selectable auto-scroll speeds, and an always-on-top keeper. It builds an honest cue.exe with its own icon, ships under GPL-3.0, and sends data only to the provider you configure.

Brand guidelines

  • Write cue for Windows on first mention, then cue.
  • Don't alter the icon or imply official endorsement by Microsoft, OpenAI, Dahl, OpenRouter or any other provider.
  • Describe screen-share exclusion as best-effort, never as guaranteed or undetectable.
  • Mention that this is a fork of Blueturboguy07/cue when describing its origin.
  • Link to the landing page or the repository.

cue is intended for notes, studying, accessibility and practice. Using a hidden assistant during a proctored exam, job interview or recorded meeting may break platform rules and local consent laws.
